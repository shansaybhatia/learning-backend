File structure for backend development:
    1. **Project Root**
        - README.md
        - package.json
        - .gitignore
        - .env
        - server.js (or app.js)
    2. **src**
        index.js
        app.js
        constants.js
        -**Database**
            - **database**
            - db.js
        - **controllers**
            - userController.js
            - authController.js
            - productController.js
        - **models**
            - userModel.js
            - productModel.js
        - **routes**
            - userRoutes.js
            - authRoutes.js
            - productRoutes.js
        - **middlewares**
            - authMiddleware.js
            - errorMiddleware.js
        - **utils**
            - logger.js
            - validator.js
    3. **config**
        - dbConfig.js
    4. **tests**
        - user.test.js
        - auth.test.js
        - product.test.js
